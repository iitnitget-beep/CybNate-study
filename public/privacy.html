Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

Welcome to CybNate ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a secure, transparent learning environment for students preparing for competitive entrance examinations like NEET and JEE. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your information when using the CybNate mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to authenticate students, secure accounts, and deliver personalized study diagnostics:

  • Google Credential Manager Authentication: When you choose Google Sign-In, we utilize the modern Android Jetpack CredentialManager API (via GetSignInWithGoogleOption). We receive your verified Google account credentials (full name, email address, and profile photo) to establish your secure user account.
  • Phone Number & SMS OTP Verification: When signing in via mobile phone number, we collect your phone number (including country code) and process one-time password (OTP) verification codes through Firebase Phone Authentication to verify your identity without requiring static passwords.
  • Real-Time Firestore Analytics & Study History: When you practice questions or complete mock tests, we record your test ID, timestamp, duration, question responses, bookmark selections, and score outcomes in Google Cloud Firestore. This data is streamed in real time to calculate your accuracy percentiles, weekly question counts, and speed metrics.
  • Device Attestation & Anti-Abuse Telemetry: We employ Firebase App Check utilizing Google Play Integrity to ensure requests originate exclusively from genuine, unmodified CybNate application installations.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to facilitate your educational experience and safeguard service integrity:

  • To authenticate your student identity via Google Sign-In or Phone OTP and protect against unauthorized account takeover.
  • To synchronize practice test sessions, bookmarked questions, and historical review data in real time across your devices via Firestore.
  • To generate dynamic analytics, including 4-week practice comparisons, question accuracy percentages, and average time-per-question benchmarks.
  • To prevent brute-force attacks and maintain rate limiting via our localized lockout mechanisms.

3. Data Protection and Third-Party Infrastructure

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. We utilize Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) infrastructure—including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Play Integrity—all hosted in secure enterprise data centers adhering to international standards (ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 1/2/3).

4. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

We retain your test attempts and account data for as long as your student account is active. You have the right to inspect, export, or permanently delete your account and all associated test records at any time. To request complete account deletion, contact our privacy officer at the address below.
